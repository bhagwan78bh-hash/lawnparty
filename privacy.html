1. Introduction & Core Commitment

This Privacy Policy describes how Lawnparty ("we", "our", or "us") manages your personal information when you access our website, read our garden guides, interact with our party planning tools, or subscribe to our newsletter dispatch.

We adhere strictly to privacy-by-design principles: we collect only the minimal data required to deliver high quality, functional guide content, and we will never monetize, broker, or sell your personal details to advertising networks.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information in two primary ways:

A. Information You Voluntarily Provide

  • Newsletter Subscriptions: If you voluntarily subscribe to the Lawnparty Host Dispatch, we collect your email address. You may unsubscribe at any time with a single click.
  • Direct Communications: If you email our editorial or legal team with questions or suggestions, we collect your sender email address and the contents of your message.

B. Automatically Collected Technical Information

  • Server Logs: Like standard web hosts, our infrastructure may log basic connection metadata including IP addresses, browser types, operating systems, referring URLs, and timestamps. This data is reviewed strictly in aggregate for server performance, capacity planning, and defending against malicious cyber activity.

3. Client-Side Local Storage & Cookies

Checklist Privacy Guarantee: When you check off party tasks in our Interactive Checklist, your selections are stored strictly inside your own device's browser memory via localStorage. This data never leaves your computer and is never transmitted to our servers.

Understanding how client storage functions on Lawnparty:

  • localStorage for Checklists: We use the browser's local key-value store to save task completion states (key: lawnparty_guide_checklist_v1) so your progress remains when you refresh the page. You can clear this at any time by clicking "Reset All" on the checklist or clearing your browser site data.
  • Absence of Third-Party Ad Cookies: Lawnparty does not install persistent third-party advertising cookies, behavioral tracking pixels, or cross-domain fingerprinting beacons.

4. How We Use Information

We use information solely for the following legitimate purposes:

  • To present, format, and render the Lawnparty guide smoothly across smartphones, tablets, and desktop computers.
  • To transmit periodic entertaining guides, seasonal recipes, and party tips to confirmed newsletter subscribers.
  • To respond promptly to visitor queries, legal correspondence, or content corrections.
  • To protect our website infrastructure from automated abuse, spam submissions, or security breaches.

5. Information Sharing & Non-Sale Policy

We do not sell, rent, trade, or commercialize your personal information to third parties.

We will only disclose personal data in the following restricted circumstances:

  • Trusted Technical Service Providers: Secure email delivery networks or web hosting infrastructure vendors acting strictly on our instructions and under binding confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Compliance: When strictly required by law, subpoena, warrant, or court order issued by an authorized government body.
  • Emergency Safety Protection: To protect the rights, property, or safety of Lawnparty, our users, or the public from severe imminent harm.

6. Data Protection & Security Measures

We implement industry-standard technical and operational safeguards to protect any stored information. All network traffic between your browser and Lawnparty is encrypted in transit using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).

While no transmission over the open Internet can be guaranteed 100% impenetrable, we review our digital infrastructure periodically to defend against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

7. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR / CCPA / CPRA)

Depending on your jurisdiction (including the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, California, and other US states), you possess specific legal rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right of Access & Portability: You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You may request correction of any inaccurate or incomplete personal records.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten"): You may request that we delete your email from our newsletter system and communication logs.
  • Right to Opt-Out: Every email newsletter contains a clear "Unsubscribe" mechanism allowing immediate self-service removal.
  • Non-Discrimination: We will never deny services, alter content quality, or charge differentiated rates for exercising your privacy rights.

To exercise any of these statutory rights, please submit a written request to privacy@lawnparty.guide. We will respond within thirty (30) days.

8. Children's Privacy Notice

Lawnparty is directed toward adult hosts, event planners, and families coordinating gatherings. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under the age of 16 without verified parental consent. If you have reason to believe that a minor has provided us with personal information, please alert us immediately at privacy@lawnparty.guide, and we will promptly purge the data from our records.

9. Data Retention Guidelines

We retain personal information only for the minimum duration necessary to satisfy the purposes delineated in this policy:

  • Newsletter Subscriptions: Retained until the recipient unsubscribes or requests deletion.
  • Communication Records: Stored for up to twelve (12) months following the resolution of your inquiry for continuity and record-keeping purposes.
  • Server Logs: Automatically rotated and purged after thirty (30) to ninety (90) days.

10. Modifications to This Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect enhancements to our guide, updates in interactive features, or changes in international data privacy regulations. When updates occur, we will revise the "Effective Date" and "Last Revised" timestamps at the top of this document. Continued use of Lawnparty following the posting of changes constitutes your agreement to the revised policy.
